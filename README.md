FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign data to understand customer response and conversion performance. The analysis focuses on conversion rates, campaign performance, contact methods, customer segments, and monthly trends.

🎯 Objective

- Analyze marketing campaign conversion performance
- Identify differences in conversion rates across contact methods
- Compare conversion performance across campaigns
- Analyze conversion trends across months
- Compare conversion rates across customer job categories
- Identify areas where marketing performance can be improved

📊 Dataset

Bank Marketing Dataset

The dataset contains information about customers contacted during a bank marketing campaign, including customer demographics, contact methods, campaign information, and whether the customer subscribed to the offered product.

The dataset is commonly known as the Bank Marketing Dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- GitHub

📈 Key Analysis

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Overall conversion rate
- Conversion rate by contact method
- Marketing conversion funnel
- Conversion rate by campaign
- Conversion rate by month
- Conversion rate by customer job category
- Customer and campaign-related analysis

💡 Key Insight

The overall campaign conversion rate in the analyzed dataset is approximately 11.7%.

Conversion performance varies depending on the contact method, campaign, month, and customer job category. These differences can help identify which marketing approaches and customer segments perform better.

🔍 Recommendations

- Focus more on contact methods that show stronger conversion performance.
- Identify campaigns with better conversion rates and study their approach.
- Analyze lower-performing campaigns to determine possible areas for improvement.
- Use customer segment information to improve campaign targeting.
- Monitor conversion performance over time to identify useful trends.

📁 Project File

⚠️ Note

The Bank Marketing dataset represents a campaign contact-to-response funnel rather than a traditional website visitor → lead → customer funnel. The analysis therefore focuses on campaign contacts and customer responses/subscriptions.
